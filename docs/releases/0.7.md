- Stop everything in a project: right-click its heading and choose Stop All. While you're filtering, it stops only the matching servers.
- Stop everything: right-click the menu bar icon and choose Stop All Servers. It lists what it will stop and asks first.
- Hidden servers are left running. A server that won't stop offers Force Quit, as it does when you stop it on its own.

Headwaiter is in beta. If it gets a server wrong, right-click the menu bar icon, choose Capture Diagnostics and [open an issue](https://github.com/individual11/headwaiter/issues). Check the capture for private details first.

Needs macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
