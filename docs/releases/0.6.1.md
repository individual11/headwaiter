- A new look for the download: open the DMG and your server will be right with you. The app itself is the same as 0.6.
- New in 0.6, if you're coming from 0.5: use the list from the keyboard, reveal folders in Finder or open them in your terminal, and keep an optional log of starts and stops.

Headwaiter is in beta. If it gets a server wrong, right-click the menu bar icon, choose Capture Diagnostics and [open an issue](https://github.com/individual11/headwaiter/issues). Check the capture for private details first.

Needs macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
